Port Emm
Zavidovo

A resort identity shaped by life on the Volga. Developed with DUGA for Samolet Select.

Art direction — Anna Dmitrieva / Samolet
Brand identity — in collaboration with DUGA

A distinct character.
A shared brand language.

As art director on the Samolet side, I worked with DUGA on the identity for Port Emm Zavidovo. The task was to give the resort its own character within the Samolet Select brand.

Life by the water.

Airy typography, natural tones and a vivid orange accent connect the identity to the energy of resort life. The system carries across outdoor and digital communication.

Identity you can hold.

A quieter expression of the same system: tactile print, considered finishes and small details.

From communication
to the resort itself.

Wayfinding and physical applications bring the identity into the spaces and experiences of Port Emm.
