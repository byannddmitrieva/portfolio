CENTRUS

CENTRUS is a premium residential quarter in the historic centre of Riga, bringing together contemporary architecture, private terraces, a landscaped courtyard, residences and commercial spaces. I designed the project’s website, translating its understated architectural character into a clear digital experience — from information architecture and visual direction to apartment search, filtering, finish selection and responsive mobile journeys.
