ACTEAVE

Brand identity and packaging for a tea brand concept for the US market. A visual system shaped around the rhythms of everyday life.

Brand identity & packaging — Anna Dmitrieva & Anastasia Ashukina
2022

Tea for the way
you live.

The identity connects green tea, botanical imagery and everyday rituals. A fluid symbol, serif lettering and an olive accent give the brand its natural character.

Four moments.
One visual language.

Morning Rhythm, Midday Focus, Afternoon Boost and Evening Chill. Distinct botanical illustrations help each flavor stand out, while the triangular motif and clean white layout hold the collection together.

One collection.
All day.

Time Edition brings three teas into one assortment. The olive frame and a consistent hierarchy connect the front and back of the packaging.

One brand.
Different moods.

Time Edition, Gym Collection and Love Edition extend the same system through different themes, illustrations and accent colors.
