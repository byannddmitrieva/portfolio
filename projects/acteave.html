Acteave

Tea for the way you live. Brand identity and packaging for a tea brand concept created for the US market.

Brand Identity · Logo · Packaging Design / 2022

01 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

Tea that follows
your rhythm.

Acteave is a tea brand concept for the US market. Its identity and packaging bring together green tea, botanical imagery and the rituals of everyday life.

The task was to create a recognizable visual system that connects every individual flavor with the wider collection.

Brand identity · Logo · Packaging design

02 / BRAND CONCEPT

Every moment
has its own flavor.

From morning rituals to evening pauses. Names, colors and illustrations help people choose a tea for their mood and time of day.

03 / BRAND IDENTITY

Character in a single letter.

A fluid symbol and elegant serif lettering give the brand a soft, natural character. An olive accent connects the symbol and lettering into a unified brand signature.

04 / GRAPHIC LANGUAGE

A small shape.
A world within.

The triangle opens a window into the character of each tea. Botanical illustrations and distinct color combinations sit within a clean white layout.

10 / TIME EDITION

One collection. All day.

Three teas. Three moments.

The front brings the collection together. An olive frame unifies the layout, while botanical triangles make each tea easy to recognize.

Morning — a gentle start.
Midday — a moment to pause.
