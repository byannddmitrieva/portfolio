The new layer
of fashion.

TRY1 lives in the gap between discovering fashion online and trying it on in real life. The identity had to connect both worlds: bold in fashion, precise in infrastructure, human at every touchpoint.

A familiar face.
An open identity.

The mascot is inspired by a mannequin, a familiar object from the fashion world. No gender, age or cultural codes: the white head and two vertical eyes never change, the clothes always do.

Four levels of presence

Each version has its own job. The bigger the format and the moment, the more of the character you see.

Full body

Campaigns, storytelling, social media and spatial activations.

Head only

Retail environments, installations, window displays and bold brand moments.

Illustration

Digital communication, app, functional content and small formats.

Eyes only

Subtle branding and details: tags, packaging, a minimal TRY1 signature.

The face stays. Fashion changes.

Streetwear, tailoring, sport or a partner collection: styling is the variable, the head is the constant.

Constants

Mascot head · Typography · Technical data · Frame system

Variables

Fashion · Partner · Location · Accent colour · Campaign imagery

Fashion
happens here.

Rooted in fashion, never traditional fashion advertising. Clean editorial images, the real streets of Milan and one unexpected detail. I art-directed the image world and its rules.

Editorial & street

Contemporary, confident and slightly unconventional, simple enough for the graphic system to live around it.

One image. One colour focus.

Neutral by default, colour by intention: blue or dust yellow appears through clothing, objects or light, never both at once.

Clear focus. Room to breathe.

One subject per image. Negative space leaves room for typography, data and the frame.

Four image types

A clear brief for every shoot and every AI-generated scene.

From first click
to the fitting room.

The identity carried into the digital product: one brand, two ways in, for shoppers and for partner boutiques. The website was designed and built by the TRY1 team on this system.

A place
to connect.

Fitting points and lockers turn a functional service into a recognisable place: bold type, unit IDs, technical data and the corner frame, adapted to each architecture.

Spatial identity

A fitting point in a container: unit ID and coordinates on the façade, the corner frame and a clean interior.

Locker system

Clean structure, selected blue doors, large-scale branding and the unit ID of each location.

Present, not dominant.

TRY1 joins the partner’s store without taking it over: a blue signal in the window and a recognisable fitting zone inside, while the partner brand stays dominant.

Storefronts

Blue accents and corner markers: TRY1 is available here.

Window displays

The head-only mascot as a bold window moment.

Inside partner stores

Modular structures, branded furniture and selected colour accents create a TRY1 zone.

Recognition outside.
Discovery inside.

Packaging is part of the experience, not just a container: clean and functional on the outside, colour, messaging and small surprises inside. Every layer carries the system, from logo and data to tone of voice.

Paper bags

Try1 Blue, Dust Yellow or neutral: bold colour, minimal branding and technical details at pickup.

Boxes

A white exterior, a Try1 Blue interior with a message.

Packaging tape

Logo, unit ID and short functional messages repeat along the tape: OPEN · TRY · RETURN.

Business cards

Front: the mascot face out of focus, with the wordmark and coordinates. Back: name, role, contacts and a QR code to try1.it.

A character to carry.

Merch is where the identity plays: oversized type, mascot illustrations and freer interpretations of the name on limited editions, while the official logo stays unchanged.

Creative totes

Mascot illustrations, expressive TRY1 lettering and campaign messages.

Mascot keychains

The character as a collectible object: tags, colour accents and location details.

A character with
a social life.

On social, the mascot lives in Milan: it rides the metro, visits partner stores, floats in a pool. Spontaneous and slightly playful, closer to street photography than to campaign imagery.

The city is our canvas.

Outside conventional formats: bus shelters, crossings, building wraps and billboards around fashion locations. One character, one blue accent, a familiar city context, made to be photographed and shared.

Digital out-of-home

An anamorphic screen where the head breaks out of the frame.

Beyond the expected.

Brand moments people can step into: a mascot pavilion during fashion week, an oversized figure in the city, a TRY1 Blue light tunnel and a mascot photo booth.
