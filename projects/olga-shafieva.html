Two disciplines.
One visual language.

Real estate and art consultancy meet through the person behind the expertise. Portraits, expressive typography and architectural photography establish a coherent personal presence.

Real estate.
A personal perspective.

Portraits alternate with information to set the pace. A personal story introduces the expertise and approach behind the services.

From expression
to a clear structure.

Buying and selling follow the same grid: stages of work on the left, interior photography on the right. A repeatable composition makes the information easy to navigate.

Art as an extension
of space.

The second chapter shifts the mood. Sculpture, vivid blue and red accents introduce art consultancy, while a shared typographic system connects the two disciplines.

Different needs.
A coherent presentation.

Architecture, interiors and art come together in one story. Large headlines, contrasting photographs and quiet text blocks give the presentation a consistent rhythm.
