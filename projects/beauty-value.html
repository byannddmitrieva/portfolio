Beauty &
Value

A presentation for Marina Roshchakhovskaya on beauty and value in real estate. Architecture, typography and data, brought together in one visual story.

Presentation Design · Art Direction
2026

Open any image to view it at full size.

Beauty.
And what lies beyond.

The presentation opens with a question about the difference between an appealing image and a property’s value. Architectural photography and contrasting typography introduce the theme from the first slide.

Questions set
the pace.

Large statements, contrasting typefaces and photographs within the lines turn the talk into a visual narrative. Each question leads to the next.

Data as part
of the story.

Market figures become contrasting compositions. Large numbers, a clear hierarchy and alternating light and dark layouts bring the key points into focus.

Different properties.
Distinct stories.

From Jomana and One Tower to Novaya Riga and Khamovniki, each example has its own visual emphasis. A shared grid and typographic language connect them into a coherent talk.

Expressive form.
A clear message.

The final chapter returns to responsibility and the criteria of value. Contrasting imagery and a quiet structure carry the presentation’s visual language through to the end.
