PERVERT

An independent fashion label built from positioning to a complete identity, product language and retail expression.

Creative Direction & Brand Design
Positioning · Identity · Art Direction · Packaging · Retail

Visit website

Identity with an attitude.

PERVERT was developed as a complete brand system: a sharp wordmark, a recognisable symbol and a monochrome visual language designed to work across garments, packaging and communication.

Material becomes the message.

The identity moves onto product through metallic finishes, labels and intentionally raw details. Each application keeps the brand visible without turning it into decoration.

Campaign as fashion image.

Art direction balances provocation with control: direct portraits, dark styling and restrained compositions that give the garments space.

From product to place.

The same visual logic extends into retail and digital touchpoints, creating a consistent experience from first contact to the physical boutique.
